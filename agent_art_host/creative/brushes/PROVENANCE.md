The two native MyPaint presets come from Krita 5.2.13 with settings unchanged;
line endings and trailing whitespace are normalized for source control.
Their embedded comments identify David Revoy's brushkit V6 and CC0-1.0:

- `pencil.myb`: Pencil 2B (`c)_Pencil_2b_(mypaint).myb`).
- `wet-paint.myb`: Basic Digital Brush Smudging (`i)_Wet_Paint_Plus_(mypaint).myb`).

libmypaint is loaded from an installed runtime, not redistributed here.
